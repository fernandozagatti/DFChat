# Datasets summary

We use five publicly healthcare datasets from the Kaggle: 

* [Human Stress Prediction](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kreeshrajani/human-stress-prediction)
* [Fetal Health Classification](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/andrewmvd/fetal-health-classification) 
* [Diabetes Health Indicators Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/alexteboul/diabetes-health-indicators-dataset) 
* [Lung Cancer Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/akashnath29/lung-cancer-dataset) 
* [Suicide and Depression Detection](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nikhileswarkomati/suicide-watch).
